# Incident response

Severity is assigned within the first ten minutes of an incident.

- Sev 1: customer-facing outage. Page the duty operator and the account lead immediately.
- Sev 2: degraded service with a workaround. Open a bridge and keep the log with the incident record.
- Sev 3: internal only. Handle during the next business shift.

Every incident ends with a short retrospective filed against the service record.
